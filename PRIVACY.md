# YouFly Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026  
**App:** YouFly (iOS and Android)  
**Developer:** Van Nguyen Duc

This Privacy Policy explains how YouFly handles information when you play the game.

## Information collected in the game

YouFly is designed to be played without creating an account or signing in. The current game does not ask players to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or other personal information to play.

The current version is not intended to include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that collect personal information on behalf of the developer.

## Game progress

YouFly can save game-related information, such as a best score, locally on your device to support gameplay. This game progress is not intended to be sent to the developer's servers.

## Sharing your score

If you choose the **Share Score** feature, the sharing service or app that you select handles the information you decide to share according to that service's privacy practices. Sharing is initiated by the player.

## Permissions

Core gameplay does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or personal files.

## TestFlight and app stores

If you install a beta through Apple TestFlight or download YouFly through an app store, Apple or the relevant app store may process download information, crash reports, diagnostics, and feedback according to their own terms and privacy policies. Information that you deliberately send in a support email may be used to respond to your request.

## Children's privacy

YouFly does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through the gameplay experience.

## Sale and sharing of personal data

The developer does not sell personal information collected through YouFly. The game is not designed to share players' personal information with advertisers.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if YouFly's features, advertising, analytics, or data practices change. Changes will be published here with an updated date.
