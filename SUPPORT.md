## Common questions

### How do I play?

Open YouFly, choose a male or female character, tap **Start Flying**, and tap the screen to fly through obstacles. Try to improve your best score.

### Do I need an account?

No account or sign-in is required to play.

### What if there is no sound?

Check your device's volume and sound settings. If the issue continues, email support with your exact device model and iOS version. Audio behavior is being verified across iPhone models.

### What if the game feels slow or the screen shakes?

Please contact support and provide the device model, OS version, and when the issue happens. This will help us reproduce the problem.

### How do I share a score?

When a flight ends, use the **Share Score** option if it is available on your device.
