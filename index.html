YouFly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouFly handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The current version of YouFly does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal files.

Game Data

YouFly may store game-related information, such as your best score, locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer.

Permissions

YouFly does not intentionally request access to sensitive device permissions such as contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or personal files for its core gameplay.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version of YouFly does not include third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal information on behalf of the developer.

If advertising, analytics, online services, or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices and third-party services.

Children's Privacy

YouFly does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the game's features or data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or share personal information collected through YouFly because the current version of the game does not directly collect personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when YouFly's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
